      How it works

      1. Click the icon, then point.

        Whatever is under the cursor lights up, including elements inside iframes and open Shadow DOM.

      2. Press Space for hover menus.

        A click picks the element. Space or Enter picks it without moving the mouse, so the menu stays open.

      3. Copy the one on top.

        Copying keeps the panel open and drops the locator into the test box, so you can check it on the spot.

      4. After a deploy, check them all.

        Paste your suite’s selectors into Heal, or the page object itself, and verify them in one go. Broken ones get a suggested fix. Scan lists every control on the page with its best locator.
